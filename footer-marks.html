FOOTER STUDY · BUILT FROM THE MARKS

The same five shapes, made into a park.

Every form here is one of the five category marks — square, circle, triangle, diamond, cross — at the proportions the project map draws them. Towers are squares, the wheel carries one of each mark around its rim, the canopy is a fan of triangles, the mounts and the coaster cars are marks too, and the crowd is a scatter of all five. Only the structure is drawn as line. Switch to dark and the lamps come on.
