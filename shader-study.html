SHADER STUDY · 01

Hover, and the paint crumbles off the picture.

Move the cursor over a card and the patch of surface under it breaks into tiny category marks — the project's own symbol — which drift outward, shrink and fade, letting the project's own screenshot show through the hole. The dissolve front is ordered-dithered, so the edge breaks up in a Bayer pattern instead of cutting a clean circle.

The pieces are the same marks the project map draws, at the same proportions. A cell only renders as a piece while it is mid-flight — intact cells are still sheet, and finished ones are gone — so the cost stays in the crumbling ring rather than the whole card. Each fragment checks a 5×5 neighbourhood, because a piece drifts over its neighbours.

GRAIN sets the cell size: small is a fine sand, large reads as torn paper. REACH is how far the hole opens, DRIFT how far pieces travel before they fade. Turning DITHER off replaces the Bayer threshold with a flat one, which is the quickest way to see what the dithering is doing to the edge.
