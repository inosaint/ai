SPECIMEN SHEET · KENNETH'S AI PLAYGROUND

Three drawings, one dataset, no images.

Everything below is generated in the browser at run time — every line, colour plane and figure built in JS from the same 43-project record set. Nothing here is an image asset. The theme switch drives all three at once; the two parks redraw in a slightly different hand on every seed.

Project map

SPECIMEN 01 · grid-viz.js

One mark per project, one column per month, shape by kind. Gaps are months where nothing shipped; the dashed ring marks the current month. The hero of the playground page runs this same function at a smaller cell size with the legend suppressed.

AS IT APPEARS IN THE HERO — SMALL, NO LEGEND

The park — painted

SPECIMEN 02 · park.js

Loose, hand-drawn, colour-block. The wheel turns, cars run the coaster, and the carousel spins — its horses turning about their poles as they round each end so they always lead with the head, and its far side passing behind the drum. Switch to dark and the lamps come on.

The park — elevation

SPECIMEN 03 · park-arch.js

The same park redrawn as a measured architectural elevation: orthographic, hairline draughting, flat colour planes sitting behind the linework like cut paper, construction lines running off the sheet. Dark mode reads it as a blueprint.
